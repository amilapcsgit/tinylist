Overview

NeonList is an offline-first list management Android application. NeonList does not require an account, login, registration, subscription, or cloud service for core functionality.

NeonList does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data.

Local App Data

User-created lists, list items, notes, quantities, and related app content are stored locally on the user's Android device.

Data is not sent to PCSLanka or to a developer-operated server.

Android Permissions

NeonList does not intentionally request sensitive Android permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, or phone state.

Data Retention and User Control

User data remains on the device until the user deletes it inside the app, clears app storage through Android settings, or uninstalls the app.

If the user manually exports, copies, screenshots, backs up, or transfers app data using Android or third-party tools, that exported data is controlled by the user.

Security

Security of locally stored data depends on the user's Android device security, including device lock, Android OS security, encryption, backups, and access controls.

Children's Privacy

NeonList is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

PCSLanka may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or NeonList, contact PCSLanka at amilaprasadpcs@gmail.com.
